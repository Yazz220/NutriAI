# Nosh Product and Engineering Research Artifact

This artifact records product decisions, implementation experiments, and supporting evidence for the Nosh recipe-to-cookbook experience.

## Layers

- `logic/` — crystallized product and engineering knowledge
- `src/` — implementation references and configuration context
- `trace/` — decisions, experiments, pivots, and session history
- `evidence/` — raw supporting artifacts
- `staging/` — observations awaiting a closure signal
