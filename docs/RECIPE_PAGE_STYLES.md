# Recipe page styles

The creation Studio offers three book-owned page languages: Illustrated, Editorial, and Heritage. Each has two generated sample pages under `assets/cookbook/style-previews/`. The Cover and Inside preview switcher shows those samples as a live spread when the user selects a style.

The 4:5 contract applies to the finished page canvas, not to the food photograph or illustration placed inside the layout. Each style may compose its internal imagery differently within that fixed page. The shared complete-page prompt and provider request own the canvas ratio; the three style profiles only control visual language and composition.

## Shared sample-page prompt

Generate one finished, flat, portrait 4:5 cookbook recipe page. It must look like a polished published page, not a photographed book, device mockup, loose paper, or multi-page spread. Keep every supplied line accurate and readable, with generous symmetric safe margins and no logos, watermarks, hands, utensils covering text, or extra recipe claims.

Brownie copy: “Fudgy Chocolate Brownies — Deep chocolate brownies with glossy tops and soft centers. Bake 25 min · Makes 16.” Ingredients: 170 g butter; 200 g dark chocolate; 200 g sugar; 3 eggs; 95 g flour; 25 g cocoa; 1 tsp vanilla; 1/2 tsp salt. Method: melt butter and chocolate; whisk sugar, eggs, and vanilla; fold in dry ingredients; bake at 175°C for 25 minutes; cool before slicing.

Cookie copy: “Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies — Chewy centers, crisp edges and toasted caramel depth. Prep 20 min · Bake 12 min · Makes 12.” Ingredients: 140 g butter; 150 g brown sugar; 50 g white sugar; 1 egg plus 1 yolk; 1 tsp vanilla; 190 g flour; 1/2 tsp baking soda; 170 g dark chocolate; 1/2 tsp salt. Method: brown and cool butter; whisk with sugars, egg, yolk, and vanilla; fold in dry ingredients and chocolate; portion and chill; bake at 180°C for 10–12 minutes.

## Illustrated

Final visual direction: refined black-ink food drawing with delicate translucent watercolor; pristine warm alabaster paper; muted sage and ochre accents; elegant warm serif title; calm highly legible recipe type; airy contemporary cookbook composition with generous margins and one integrated illustration.

Assets: `illustrated-brownies.png`, `illustrated-cookies.png`.

## Editorial

Final visual direction: premium contemporary culinary editorial photography; realistic overhead food image with natural texture; clean warm-white paper; confident high-contrast serif title; precise sans-serif recipe text and uppercase labels; disciplined asymmetric grid; restrained terracotta rules and generous whitespace.

Assets: `editorial-brownies.png`, `editorial-cookies.png`.

## Heritage

Final visual direction: refined engraved copperplate food artwork with controlled cross-hatching; pristine warm parchment without fake stains or distress; deep umber ink and restrained antique-gold rules; dignified serif typography; balanced columns, quiet classical ornament, generous margins, and modern legibility.

Assets: `heritage-brownies.png`, `heritage-cookies.png`.
