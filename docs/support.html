Help and support

Get help with your account, recipe captures, generated pages, privacy, or a technical problem.

Contact support

For general, non-sensitive feedback, open a request in the public Folio support tracker. Include the app version, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened.

For billing, account, or privacy help, use the private support contact opened from Folio Settings. Never post receipts, transaction IDs, account identifiers, recipe photos, or other private information in the public tracker.

Do not post your password, authentication tokens, email address, private recipe content, or personal photos in a public request.

Account deletion

Open Folio, go to Settings, then choose Delete account. The app asks for confirmation before permanently deleting the account and its associated cookbooks and recipe pages. Account deletion does not cancel Folio Plus; use Manage subscription in Folio or Apple subscription settings to cancel renewal.

Folio Plus and purchases

Open Settings to view your plan, see your remaining designed-page creations, restore an existing purchase, or manage your subscription. If a purchase completed but Plus does not appear, stay signed in to the same Folio and Apple accounts, choose Restore purchases, and try again online.

AI data choices

Open Settings and choose AI data use to review or withdraw permission for external AI processing. Folio will ask again before the next AI request if you withdraw permission.

Report a Folio response

Choose Report beneath a completed Folio response, then confirm Send report. The response text is sent privately to Folio support for safety and quality review. It is not posted to the public support tracker.

Recipe capture problems

Check your connection, return to recipe activity, and use Try again. If a link cannot be read, paste the recipe text instead. Photo access is optional.

Privacy

Read the Folio privacy policy for information about stored cookbook data, external AI providers, retention, and deletion.
