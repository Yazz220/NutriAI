Help and support

Get help with your account, recipe captures, generated pages, privacy, or a technical problem.

Contact support

Open a request in the public Nosh support tracker. Include the app version, iOS version, what you expected, and what happened.

Do not post your password, authentication tokens, email address, private recipe content, or personal photos in a public request.

Account deletion

Open Nosh, go to Settings, then choose Delete account. The app asks for confirmation before permanently deleting the account and its associated cookbooks and recipe pages.

AI data choices

Open Settings and choose AI data use to review or withdraw permission for external AI processing. Nosh will ask again before the next AI request if you withdraw permission.

Recipe capture problems

Check your connection, return to recipe activity, and use Try again. If a link cannot be read, paste the recipe text instead. Photo access is optional.

Privacy

Read the Nosh privacy policy for information about stored cookbook data, external AI providers, retention, and deletion.
