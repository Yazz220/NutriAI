Nosh

Privacy policy

Effective August 31, 2026

Nosh is a personal cookbook with an AI chef. This policy explains what information Nosh handles and the choices available to you.

Information Nosh handles

  • Account information. Your email address, user ID, authentication provider, and any name returned by Sign in with Apple.
  • Cookbook content. Cookbook titles and styles, recipes, recipe links, pasted text, photos, video links, RecipeGraphs, generated pages, and import history.
  • Conversation content. Messages sent to Nosh, attached images, and the active cookbook or recipe context needed to answer.
  • AI response reports. When you choose Report beneath a Nosh response, Nosh stores that response text, its device-local message identifier, your user ID, and report status for private safety and quality review.
  • Purchase information. Your Nosh plan, subscription product, renewal state, purchase and expiration dates, and an app-specific customer identifier. Nosh does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Technical information. Request timing, errors, service logs, IP address, device or operating-system information made available to infrastructure providers, and local app state used to restore your session and cookbooks.

How Nosh uses information

Nosh uses this information to create and secure your account, save and sync cookbooks, extract recipes, generate cookbook pages, answer cooking questions, restore unfinished work, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and delete your data when you ask.

External AI processing

Nosh asks for permission before it sends personal content to external AI.

If you allow AI processing, recipe links, pasted text, photos, video links, chat messages, and relevant cookbook or recipe context may be sent to OpenRouter. OpenRouter routes requests to the selected Qwen model provider for recipe extraction, conversation, and page generation.

OpenRouter states that it does not train on API inputs or outputs, but model providers have their own retention and training practices. Review the OpenRouter privacy policy and its linked model-provider information before allowing AI processing. Do not submit another person's private information or image without their permission.

You can decline AI processing and continue reading saved cookbooks and managing your account. You can withdraw permission from Settings under AI data use. Withdrawing permission stops new AI requests on that device; it does not undo processing that already occurred.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and Edge Function infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat receives an app-specific user identifier and App Store purchase information so Nosh can verify Nosh Plus, restore purchases, and keep access in sync across supported devices.
  • OpenRouter and selected model providers process content only after you allow AI processing.
  • Apple processes Sign in with Apple when you choose that login method and processes App Store purchases.

Nosh does not sell your personal information. Nosh does not use your cookbook or conversation content for advertising.

Storage and retention

Account and cookbook records remain in cloud storage while your account is active. Recipe source media is retained so you can review and retry imports. Generated cookbook pages remain until you delete the related content or account. AI response reports remain while needed for safety review and follow-up, or until your account is deleted. Conversation threads and cookbook caches are also stored on your device.

Generated page images are stored privately and delivered through short-lived links after Nosh verifies your signed-in account. Avoid placing sensitive personal information in recipe content. Infrastructure and AI providers may retain service data under their own policies or legal obligations.

Your choices

  • Decline or withdraw external AI processing.
  • Decline photo access and use pasted text or recipe links instead.
  • Delete individual recipes and cookbooks in the app.
  • Delete your account from Settings. Account deletion removes the account, cookbooks, captures, generated page objects, subscription record held by Nosh, and the app-specific RevenueCat customer record.

Deleting your Nosh account does not cancel an App Store subscription or remove Apple's transaction records. Manage or cancel Nosh Plus from your Apple subscription settings before deleting your account if you do not want it to renew.

Children

Nosh is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account or send personal information to Nosh if local law does not permit you to do so.

Security

Nosh uses authentication, access controls, encrypted network connections, and user-scoped storage paths. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will appear on this page with a new effective date. Nosh may ask for permission again if a change affects how external AI receives personal content.

Contact and privacy requests

Use the Nosh support page for help or privacy questions. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens, recipe photos, or other sensitive information in a public support request.
